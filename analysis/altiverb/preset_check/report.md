# Altiverb factory presets rendered through the plugin, against their own Altiverb IR

Generated by `tools/altiverb_preset_check.py` (`--stage all`). Every preset rendered with `REV246Render` at its snapped panel values (`presets.json`), 48 kHz (the IRs' rate), `mod=1`, **`sixteen=0`**, impulse at 0 dBFS, 50 samples of engine latency removed (the same for every render). `sixteen=1` (the plugin default) puts a -96 dBFS quantisation floor under the tail that wrecks a Schroeder T60 of an impulse render (T60 reads 1-2 s for a 5 s decay); the 16-bit render of every non-N preset is kept in `measure.json` (`render16on`) and the check is therefore not valid at `sixteen=1`. M / L / E are time-variant: T60 and EDT are the mean of 3 renders at different input phases, so they are statistics (jitter in the table), never nulls.

**56 of 64 presets pass** every check (no flag); 8 flagged. Family presets (the calibration): 31 of 33 pass.

Metrics are judged **family-relative**: per program the median over its family presets of each log ratio (render / IR) is the 'chain bias' and is removed (Altiverb 8 kHz anomaly, its low-band chain, level conventions). Chain bias of T60, 125 Hz-8 kHz, % (S / M / L / E): S -0.1 +0.0 -0.1 +0.0 -0.0 +0.2 +0.8; M -0.7 -0.4 -0.6 -0.1 -0.4 -0.4 +0.1; L +0.5 -0.2 +0.1 -0.5 -0.1 -0.7 +0.0; E +1.2 +2.2 -0.3 +1.5 +1.1 +0.8 -0.5.

Flag thresholds (the snapping step): **RT** = mean of 500 Hz-2 kHz T60 miss > half the RT table step's ratio in the direction of the miss; **LF** = 125 / 250 Hz miss after the RT part > half the LF step's ratio; **HF** = 2 / 4 kHz likewise (8 kHz never counted); **AMPL** = implied AMPL (set x 10^(-dB/20), family-relative cluster-tap / reverb ratio) differs from the set value by > 5; **DELAY** = onset difference > 0.5 ms; **LOWPASS** = a candidate in {6, 8, 10, 12} kHz fits the tap-0 shape (1-12 kHz, relative to the family reference, level removed) better than the set one by > 1 dB rms; **N** = a neighbouring (SPAN, EARLY, LATE) rendered by the plugin lowers the 5 ms band-envelope residual by > 0.5 dB and 15 %.

## Systematic findings

- **M and L: the plugin's cluster / reverb ratio is 0.4 dB (M) / 0.2 dB (L) below the Altiverb's family presets at the same AMPL**, S and E are within 0.6 / 0.4 dB. Not Altiverb-specific: the same measure on Tomkin M files gives the plugin -9 to -12 dB against the hardware capture at the same settings (M: all four cluster taps 3.75x smaller relative to the 40-120 ms reverb RMS, constant over AMPL 10-99 and RT / LF / HF), while on Tomkin S it is a constant +2.3 dB. The family-relative method hides it; absolute AMPL readings in M / L are therefore not comparable to Tomkin's. Not a preset error, a plugin/engine level question (reverb level against cluster in M and L).
- **Numbered presets carry a common AMPL offset against the family** (render minus IR, dB, median): S +0.0 (range -0.5..+0.3, n=5), M -0.7 (range -1.4..+0.8, n=8), L -0.5 (range -1.3..-0.3, n=8), E -0.2 (range -0.8..-0.1, n=6). Every numbered S / M / L preset has the plugin's cluster louder than the IR's, relative to the family; E is within about 1 dB. Either the numbered AMPLs are all high relative to the family anchors (80 / 80 / 60), or the anchors are low. The column 'AMPL vs numbered median' removes it to show the scatter.
